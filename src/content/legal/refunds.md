# Refunds and cancelling

Last updated: 16 November 2026

## Cancelling is not the same as a refund

- **Cancelling** stops future payments. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
- **A refund** returns money you have already paid. Access to that purchase ends when the refund is made.

## How to cancel a subscription

Plus and Creator can be cancelled at any time in your Whop account. If you get stuck, email lms.is@pm.me and we will cancel it for you.

The Gameweek Pass and Founder Lifetime are single payments. There is nothing to cancel.

## When we will refund you

**If something is wrong.** If you paid and did not get what the plan promises, and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund you. This applies to every plan.

**If you were charged by mistake.** Duplicate payments and charges after a cancellation are refunded in full.

**If you change your mind.** Tell us within 14 days of your first payment for a plan. If you have not downloaded a paid image in that time, we refund in full. If you have, we do not normally refund, because you have used what you bought.

**Renewals.** Monthly renewals are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time to stop the next one. Yearly renewals can be cancelled within 14 days of the renewal date for a refund of the unused part.

**Founder Lifetime.** The same 14-day change-of-mind rule applies.

**Features marked "Coming later".** A feature that is marked "Coming later" and has not arrived is not a fault, because the plan was sold without it.

## Your legal rights

Nothing on this page reduces rights the law gives you where you live. If the law where you live gives you a right to cancel or to a refund, we will honour it. Tell us and we will sort it out.

## How to ask for a refund

1. Email lms.is@pm.me. Tell us the name on your Whop account, which plan you bought and the date you paid.
2. Or ask through Whop, from your Whop account.

We aim to reply within 3 working days.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through Whop to the card or method you paid with. We cannot refund by any other route. Refunds usually arrive within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

## Disputes with your bank

Please email us before you raise a dispute with your bank. A dispute takes longer than a refund, and once one is open we can no longer refund the payment ourselves.
