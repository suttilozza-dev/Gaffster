# Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 November 2026

## The short version

We keep as little about you as we can. We do not run adverts, we do not use tracking or analytics tools, and we do not sell your data. Whop handles sign-in and payment, so we never see your card details.

## 1. Who is responsible

Gaffster is run by Laurence Sutcliffe, a sole trader based in New Zealand, who decides how your information is used. Contact: lms.is@pm.me.

## 2. What we hold and why

| What | Where it comes from | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Whop user ID and display name | Whop, when you sign in | To know who is signed in and which plan you have |
| Your plan, a payment reference, and when it expires | Whop, when you pay | To give you what you paid for |
| How many images you have made this week or today | Our own count | To apply plan limits |
| Whether a purchase was refunded | Whop | To end access after a refund |
| Your email address and whatever you write | You, if you email us | To reply and to deal with your request |
| Standard technical records, such as IP address and browser type | Our hosting provider | To keep the site secure and working |

When you pay, Whop sends us a payment notice. We keep only the payment reference, your Whop user ID and the plan.

The line-ups you build stay in your browser. We do not store them.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we ask you not to send us any.

## 3. Who else handles your information

| Company | What it does for us | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whop | Sign-in, checkout, payments, refunds, and hosting the website | United States |
| Neon | Stores our database | Australia (Sydney) |
| Cloudflare | Runs the network Whop uses to serve the website | Worldwide network |
| Proton | Receives the emails you send us | Switzerland |

Whop also uses your information for its own purposes under its own privacy policy, which you agree to when you create a Whop account.

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the companies above, or where the law requires it.

## 4. Sending information between countries

Gaffster is run from New Zealand and our database is in Australia. If you live in the UK, Europe, the US or elsewhere, your information will be handled outside your country.

## 5. Our legal reasons (UK and Europe)

- **To carry out our contract with you:** sign-in, plans, payments, limits and refunds.
- **Our legitimate interests:** keeping the site secure, preventing misuse, answering your emails.
- **Legal duties:** keeping records the law requires, such as tax records.

We do not use your information for marketing.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account and usage records: for as long as you use Gaffster, then up to 12 months.
- Purchase records: 7 years, because tax law requires records to be kept.
- Emails: up to 12 months after the matter is closed.

## 7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete your information, unless the law requires us to keep it;
- stop or limit how we use it.

Email lms.is@pm.me. We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office. In New Zealand it is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner. In the EU it is the authority in your country.

## 8. Children

Gaffster is not aimed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Sign-in is handled by Whop. Our database is password-protected and reached over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

## 10. Changes

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and its date. If we add analytics or advertising, we will say so here first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
