# Cookies

Last updated: 17 November 2026

## The short version

Gaffster needs a few cookies and some browser storage to work. These are always on.

Whop, which hosts the site, can also set measurement cookies to count visits and see which links and adverts bring people to Gaffster. These are off unless you agree. We ask the first time you visit, and you can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

## What we store on your device (always on)

| Name | Type | What it does | How long it stays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mdxi_session | Cookie | Keeps you signed in. Holds your Whop user ID and display name, signed so it cannot be altered. | 30 days |
| mdxi_oauth | Cookie | Used for a few minutes while you sign in with Whop, to check the sign-in really came from you. | Deleted when sign-in finishes |
| mdxi_anon_used | Browser storage | Remembers that you have used your free first image. | Until you clear your browser storage |
| mdxi_resume | Browser storage (this tab only) | Holds your line-up while you sign in, so you do not lose it. | Removed when you return, or when you close the tab |
| tsr-scroll-restoration-v1_3 | Browser storage (this tab only) | Remembers where you had scrolled to on a page. | Until you close the tab |
| mdxi_lineup | Browser storage | Remembers the last line-up you built, so it is still there when you come back. | Until you clear your browser storage |
| mdxi_consent | Browser storage | Remembers whether you agreed to measurement cookies. | Until you clear your browser storage |
| __cf_bm | Cookie | Helps protect the site from bots. Set by Cloudflare, which Whop uses to serve the site. | 30 minutes |

## Measurement cookies (only if you agree)

These come from Whop's measurement tool. They are only set after you choose Accept.

| Name | Type | What it does | How long it stays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _wuid, _wuid_link | Cookie | A random ID that lets Whop recognise your browser on later visits and link visits to adverts and links. | Up to 2 years (many browsers limit this to about 13 months) |
| _wpd | Cookie | A test to find which website address the cookies can be set on. | Removed straight away |
| _wuid, _wfp, _wsc_probed_at | Browser storage | Copies of the random ID and a record of details of your device, used to recognise it again. | Until you clear your browser storage, or withdraw consent |
| _wsc_probed | Browser storage (this tab only) | A short-lived check made by the measurement tool. | Until you close the tab |
| __cf_bm | Cookie, set on Whop's measurement address (t.whop.tw) | Set by Cloudflare to protect Whop's measurement service from bots. | 30 minutes |

When you choose Accept, the measurement tool sends Whop the pages you visit, the page that sent you, your browser type, language and time zone. If your browser already has a Facebook advertising cookie (_fbp), the tool reads it.

If you withdraw your consent, we delete the measurement cookies and storage we can reach. The __cf_bm cookie belongs to Whop's address and expires on its own within 30 minutes.

## Whop's cookies

When you sign in or pay, you are on Whop's pages. Whop sets its own cookies there, under its own cookie policy. We do not control them.

## Turning cookies off

You can change your choice about measurement cookies at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block ours, you will not be able to sign in or stay signed in. If your browser also blocks storage, you will not be able to make your free first image either.

## If this changes

If we add any other analytics or advertising tools, we will update this page first and ask for your consent before setting anything that is not strictly needed.
